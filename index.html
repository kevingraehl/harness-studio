Keeps boxes and pins fixed. Expands bundles to show individual wires; bundle membership stays saved. Undo restores your previous routes.

Moves every component and redraws bundles. Keeps Bundle view. Each lucky click tries another arrangement. Undo restores the previous one.

Command/Ctrl-click or drag-select first. Group boxes moves them together; Bundle wires combines selected wires. Ungroup preserves all components and wires.

Auto-arrange boxes and right-angle wires. Original project is backed up. Crossings are not junctions.

Best visibility: dark canvas + grid for pale wires; white canvas + grid for printed schematics. Wire contrast halos stay automatic.

  2. Select Wire, then click two pin circles.
  3. Select anything to edit it at right.
  4. Drag the canvas to pan; wheel to zoom.
